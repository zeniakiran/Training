About Code Galaxy

A Little About Us

Code Galaxy is a place where students discover the exciting world of programming. Coding helps develop logical thinking and problem-solving skills. It allows young learners to turn their ideas into creative projects and become tomorrow's innovators.


In today's digital world, coding is an important skill. Learning to code helps children understand technology and improve their creativity, logical thinking, and ability to solve problems. Holistic development means helping children grow their thinking, physical, and social skills so they can interact confidently with the world around them.
